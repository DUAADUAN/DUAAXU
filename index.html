About

Duaa Xu (Yining Xu) is a multidisciplinary artist and designer working across scenography, interaction, moving image and visual design. Her practice explores the intersection of technology and theatrical space, investigating how digital media, interactive systems and visual technologies can reshape spatial experience and performance.

Through scenography, projection, real-time media and emerging technologies, she explores shifting relationships between bodies, images, environments and audiences, treating technology not simply as a tool, but as an active element in constructing performative space.

She is currently studying MA Performance: Design and Practice at Central Saint Martins, University of the Arts London, and holds a BA in Art and Technology from the Media Interaction Studio at China Academy of Art (CAA).
