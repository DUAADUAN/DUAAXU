About

Duaa Xu (Yining Xu) is a multidisciplinary artist and designer working across scenography, interaction, moving image and visual design. Her practice explores how digital media and interactive technologies reshape theatrical space and the relationships between bodies, images and audiences. She often works with projection, real-time media and spatial installations, treating technology as an active part of performance rather than simply a tool for displaying images.

She is currently studying MA Performance: Design and Practice at Central Saint Martins, University of the Arts London, and holds a BA in Art and Technology from China Academy of Art.
